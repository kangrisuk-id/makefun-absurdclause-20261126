## Judge criteria (in the brief's exact order, each scored /5, total /25)

1. **C1 Buried-clause fit** — an absurd clause that could hide deep inside a terms-of-service agreement.
2. **C2 Standalone funny** — funny on its own; the joke lands on one reading, no outside setup.
3. **C3 Boilerplate register** — sounds like real legal boilerplate until the absurdity hits.
4. **C4 Original & specific** — a concrete absurd image, not a vague shrug at the genre.
5. **C5 Tight & clean** — one clean sentence, no wasted words, no explaining its own joke.
